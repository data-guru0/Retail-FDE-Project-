# Fairness

## Position

ReturnGuard decides on *transactions*, not people, and it holds **no
protected-attribute data** — no race, gender, age, disability, nationality, or
any proxy the model or the agents can see. There is therefore no
disparate-impact monitoring *code* in this project (that is a deliberate scope
cut in CLAUDE.md). This document is the fairness approach instead.

## What the risk model can see

The Behaviour model's features (see `docs/MODEL_CARD.md`): account age, order
total, refund amount, days since order, seasonality, whether a photo was
provided, lifetime orders, return rate, prior denied returns, shared
address/device/payment fingerprint counts, category, reason code. Nothing here is
a protected attribute or an obvious proxy for one. Address is used only as a
*fingerprint hash* for ring detection (does another account share this exact
value), never as a location signal.

## Where unfairness could still enter

1. **The synthetic training data.** It's generated by `ml/generate_dataset.py`
   with an explicit process; if that process encodes a bias (e.g. "night
   submissions are riskier" could correlate with shift workers in reality), the
   model learns it. Mitigation: the generative process is small and readable, the
   MODEL_CARD lists every feature and its importance, and the model is **one
   signal** — it never decides alone.
2. **The policy text.** A category exception that's harsher than justified would
   be applied consistently by the Policy agent. Mitigation: policy is versioned,
   human-edited, and every decision records which version applied, so a
   retrospective fairness review can re-decide a cohort against a corrected
   policy (`worker/reprocess.py`).
3. **The LLM's qualitative reads.** Language models carry their own biases.
   Mitigation: the Critic reviews the Decision agent, the Governance Gate is
   deterministic, and `assist`/`auto` only auto-*approve* — the failure mode of a
   biased read is an unnecessary escalation to a human, not a silent denial.

## How it would be monitored in production

With a lawful basis to hold protected attributes for monitoring only (separate
store, not a model feature): compute approval rate, escalation rate, and
override rate by group on a rolling window; alert on a gap beyond a set
threshold; feed confirmed gaps back into the policy text and the dataset. The
`agreement_samples` and `audit_log` tables already carry everything needed to
join a decision to an outcome after the fact.
